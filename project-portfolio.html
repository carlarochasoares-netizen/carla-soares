01 / DESIGN

FAU USP Research Support Centre

Bachelor's Final-Year Project · Guaraqueçaba, Brazil

My interest in sustainable architecture began while working in the Energy Efficiency Department at OTEC, where I contributed to environmental performance studies for projects including Castelão Stadium, developed for the FIFA World Cup.

This experience became the foundation for my undergraduate final-year project: a sustainable Research Support Centre that combined architectural design, environmental performance analysis and passive design strategies to respond to the local climate and users' needs.

Professional Context

At OTEC's Energy Efficiency Department, I contributed to environmental performance studies for Castelão Stadium. This professional experience influenced the passive and sustainable design strategies adopted in the Research Support Centre.

Design Development & Environmental Analysis

Technical drawings and simulations demonstrate the relationship between design intent and environmental performance.

Project Reflection

This project consolidated my interest in sustainable architecture and demonstrated how environmental analysis can support better design decisions. It strengthened the analytical and technical skills that later supported my transition into Construction Project Management.

02 / INTERIORS

Residential Interior Design

Stefany Todaka · Professional Residential Design Project

During my professional experience in residential architecture, I developed the interior design for a newly built apartment. The project translated the client's lifestyle and functional requirements into coordinated solutions.

My responsibilities included space planning, demolition and construction documentation, lighting design, custom joinery and detailed technical coordination.

Project Reflection

This project strengthened my ability to translate client requirements into practical and buildable solutions. Developing the work from concept through detailed documentation improved my coordination, communication and problem-solving skills while reinforcing the importance of accuracy in construction documentation.
